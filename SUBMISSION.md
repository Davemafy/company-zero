# Devpost submission draft

## Company Zero

Most companies start with an organization and then give it work. Company Zero starts with the work and creates the organization required to deliver it.

Give Company Zero a mission, available capabilities, a quality floor, a latency cap, and a budget. It compiles a specialized agent organization, executes the work through inspectable tools, evaluates outcomes independently, diagnoses structural failure from raw evidence, proposes competing reorganizations, reruns each candidate on the same workload, and promotes a new organization only when it satisfies the operating constitution.

### Why Track 1

Company Zero is an automated agent-engineering system. The object being engineered is the organization: role specialization, tool allocation, control stages, and orchestration structure. The system uses failure evidence to improve that organization and reports measurable changes in quality, cost, and latency.

### Measurable results

Reference benchmark:
- Invoice controls: 70% -> 100% after adding cross-case verification.
- New operating drift: 84.6% -> 100% after a second, different structural change adds assertion verification.
- Support privacy: 60% -> 100% after adding a redaction capability.

### Reliability boundaries

The model can propose structure, diagnosis, and mutations, but it cannot fabricate evidence, set measured runtime costs, mark itself correct, or promote its own proposal. Runtime validators reject unavailable tools and ungrounded evidence IDs. Candidate organizations are actually rerun before promotion.

### AO

AO is used for the actual build/debug/reliability process. The final demo must show the real AO dashboard and total real session count. Company Zero's AO Proof view imports real session evidence and intentionally shows zero until evidence exists.
